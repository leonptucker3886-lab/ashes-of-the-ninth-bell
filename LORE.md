# VEYL — Universe Bible (v1)

## Cosmology
A god called **the Ember** burned out over the world and fell as ash. Ash in the soil, water and blood is **Ember** — the source of all magic. Calendar: **AE (After the Ember)**. Across 1000 years, people go from *praying* to the Ember, to *forging* it, to *computing* it.

## The Three Eras
| Era | Year | Game | Style | Combat | Rules of the world |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| The Kindling | ~0–60 AE | Game 2 | Character-switching | Real-time action | Magic is wild and holy. Ash-priests speak to the dead. Tribes of the Cinder Steppe, the river-kingdom of Oss. Oaths are law; names have power. |
| The Bell Age | ~512 AE | **Game 1: Ashes of the Ninth Bell** | Party-based CRPG | Turn-based | The city-state **Corrow** builds *Bell-Foundries* that ring ash out of the ground. Magic becomes industry; witches are licensed or hanged. Guilds replace churches. |
| The Lattice | ~1000 AE | Game 3 | Character-locked | Tactical/real-time hybrid | Ember is refined into **lumen** that powers orbital cities. Magic is code. The dead god is being rebuilt as an AI. |

## Cultures (Bell Age)
- **Corrow** — smog city of nine great bells. Each toll draws ash up from the earth. Rule: the Bell Synod (foundry lords).
- **Ashwitches** — old-faith women and men who burn Ember from their own blood. Outlawed unless "branded" with a Synod license.
- **Bellguard** — enforcers in brass masks; deafened at oath so the bells can't madden them.
- **Gutterfolk** — the Lower Wards; surgeons, smugglers, ash-junkies ("greyeyes").
- **The Drowned Faith** — remnants of the old priesthood, who say the bells are waking the god's corpse.

## Folk tales
- **"The Ninth Bell"** — "Eight bells for the living, the ninth for the dead. When the ninth is rung, the Ember will remember it was a god." The Synod has secretly cast it.
- **"Grandmother Cinder"** — the first witch (from Game 2's era), said to have swallowed a coal from the god's heart. Her name is Ysolde — Game 2's protagonist.
- **"The Glass Child"** — a prophecy of a mind made of light that will speak for the god (foreshadowing Game 3's AI).

Sera's sister is taken by the Synod for the Ninth Bell's "tuning". The party fights from Gallows Market → Drowned Chapel → Foundry Row → Ashwell → the Ninth Bell's foundry gate, ending in a cliffhanger.
